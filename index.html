Do / Don’t

Negative sentences

6

Use don’t + base verb to say what you do not do.

Affirmative

I operate the machine.
You use a radio.

Negative

I don’t operate the machine.
You don’t use a radio.

don’t = do not
After don’t, use the base verb.
